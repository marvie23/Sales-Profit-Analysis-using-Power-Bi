# Sales-Profit-Analysis-using-Power-Bi
The Sales &amp; Profit Analysis provides insights into sales performance, profitability, and customer trends across different categories, regions, and time periods. By analyzing key metrics such as total sales, profit margins, and customer contributions, the report highlights revenue-driving factors and areas needing improvement.

**Project Title:** Sales & Profit Analysis

### **Table of Contents**
1. Objectives
2. Tools and Methodology
3. Data Structure
4. Analysis Findings
5. Recommendations
6. Conclusion

### **Objectives**
- To analyze sales and profit trends across different dimensions.
- To identify key revenue-generating categories, states, and customers.
- To highlight areas of improvement in profitability and sales performance.
- To provide data-driven recommendations for business growth.

### **Tools and Methodology**
- **Tools Used:** Microsoft Power BI
- **Methodology:**
  - Data was extracted, cleaned, and structured for analysis.
  - Various visualizations were created to display trends in sales and profit.
  - Insights were drawn based on customer, product, and regional segmentation.

### **Data Structure**
#### **1. Date Information**
- The dataset includes date-related attributes such as:
  - **Date**: The specific transaction date.
  - **Year**: The year of the transaction.
  - **Quarter Number & Quarter**: The respective quarter of the year (Q1, Q2, etc.).
  - **Month & Month Number**: The specific month and its numeric representation.
  - **Weekday & Weekday Number**: The day of the week and its corresponding numeric value.
  - **Start of Month**: Identifies the first day of the transaction month.

### **Analysis Findings**
#### **1. Sales and Profit by Weekday**
- Displays a trend analysis of sales and profit performance across different weekdays.
- Highlights peaks and troughs in sales trends to identify optimal business days.
- Example insights:
  - Highest sales recorded on Wednesdays ($0.32M).
  - Lowest profit margins observed on Thursdays.

#### **2. Sales and Profit by Category**
- Breakdown of revenue contributions from different product categories:
  - **Technology**: $602K (27.66%)
  - **Office Supplies**: $704.5K (32.36%)
  - **Furniture**: $622.3K (28.59%)
  - **Other**: $17.9K (0.82%)
- Helps in identifying which categories are driving the most revenue and profit.

#### **3. Sales and Profit by State**
- A geographic breakdown of sales and profit across various states.
- Key insights:
  - **California** leads in sales and profit.
  - **New York and Texas** are also top-performing states.
  - Some states exhibit negative or low-profit margins, indicating potential issues.

#### **4. Sales and Profit by Customer Name**

### **Recommendations**
1. **Optimize Marketing Strategies**: Target high-performing states and weekdays for promotional campaigns.
2. **Address Low-Profit Products**: Investigate categories with negative profit margins to adjust pricing or reduce costs.
3. **Enhance Customer Engagement**: Focus on top customers while developing strategies to attract new ones.
4. **Expand in High-Growth Regions**: Consider investing more in states with high sales performance.

### **Conclusion**
The Sales & Profit Analysis provides a clear view of business performance, helping stakeholders make data-driven decisions. By acting on these insights, the company can maximize profitability and improve overall sales efficiency.
